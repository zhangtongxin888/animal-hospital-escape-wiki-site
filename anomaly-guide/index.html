Verified fact

It reacts to footsteps and sound

The official description explicitly connects the Anomaly to footsteps and sound. It does not publish an exact hearing radius, noise scale, or complete list of audible actions.

Verified fact

Every Medkit escalates the Anomaly

After every Medkit, the Anomaly becomes faster and smarter. It also adapts to behavior. This makes the Medkit counter a threat indicator as well as an objective counter.

Verified warning

Do not judge the late run by the opening minutes. The official description promises escalation after each pickup.

Not publicly confirmed

The exact adaptation rules are not published

The word “adapts” is official, but the reviewed sources do not define the underlying rules, memory duration, detection math, or specific counterplay. This guide does not turn that broad statement into invented mechanics.

Recommendation

Use a conservative sound plan

Treat avoidable noise as a possible risk and pay attention to audio cues available in the current game. Do not assume a particular button makes movement silent unless the live interface states it.

  • Learn the controls from the current in-game prompts.
  • Avoid stacking several uncertain actions when the threat is close.
  • When an approach fails, adjust one choice instead of repeating the same pattern automatically.
Recommendation

Protect decisions as pressure rises

As the count climbs, shorten the time spent deciding in exposed or unfamiliar areas. Make the next route choice before leaving a landmark you recognize, and preserve enough attention to follow live prompts at 10/10.
