Verified fact

The confirmed target is 10 Medkits

The official game description states that players must find 10 Medkits and escape. Each pickup also matters to difficulty: after every Medkit, the Anomaly becomes faster and smarter and adapts to behavior.

Not publicly confirmed

Why this guide has no fixed location list

The official sources reviewed do not publish a complete Medkit location map or confirm that locations stay fixed. A numbered room-by-room list would therefore risk sending players to invented or outdated spots.

Verification limit

Use the current run as the authority. We will add exact locations only if a reliable source can verify them.

Recommendation

Divide the run into recognizable areas

Use visible landmarks in your current session to separate searched and unsearched areas. The goal is not to name imaginary rooms; it is to stop yourself from repeating the same uncertain loop.

  • Choose a recognizable landmark before beginning a search pass.
  • After a pickup, update your count immediately.
  • Before moving on, decide which visible area remains unchecked.
  • If you lose orientation, return to a landmark you can identify instead of guessing.
Recommendation

Let the count change your pace

A useful planning model is to call 0–3 the orientation phase, 4–7 the pressure phase, and 8–10 the closing phase. These brackets are our organizational recommendation, not official thresholds.

The official fact underneath the model is continuous escalation after every Medkit. At any count, use the game in front of you rather than assuming a specific speed value.

Recommendation

When the count stalls at 9/10

Do not turn the final search into a blind sprint. Reconstruct your route from recognizable landmarks and identify which area received the weakest search. Check that area deliberately while respecting the higher late-run threat.
