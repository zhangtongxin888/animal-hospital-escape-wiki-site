Verified fact

Know the mission before you move

Animal Hospital Escape by ExoSaftor Studio is set in an abandoned animal hospital. The official objective is concise: find 10 Medkits and escape.

The threat is the Anomaly. The official description says it reacts to footsteps and sound, then becomes faster and smarter after every Medkit while adapting to behavior.

Verified mission

Mission card: 10 Medkits, one escalating Anomaly, then an escape. Treat every pickup as a change in the run.

Recommendation

Use the opening to orient yourself

Before committing to a long route, notice landmarks that are visible in your current run and remember how you reached them. This is a navigation recommendation, not a claim about a fixed hospital layout.

  • Choose simple names for visible landmarks so you can remember searched areas.
  • Keep a rough sense of the route behind you before entering another area.
  • Use the on-page 0/10 tracker to reduce mental load between pickups.
Recommendation

Reset your plan after every pickup

Because escalation after each Medkit is officially confirmed, treat a pickup as a decision point. Recheck where you came from, choose the next area, and avoid assuming the pace from the previous stage still applies.

The recommendation is to make one deliberate route choice at a time. Exact pursuit logic and detection ranges are not publicly documented in the sources reviewed.

Recommendation

Take sound seriously without inventing a stealth system

The official description confirms reactions to footsteps and sound. It does not document exact hearing distance, sound meters, or a guaranteed silent movement method.

  • Read the controls and prompts shown in your current game session.
  • Assume unnecessary noise may matter, but do not rely on an unverified silent technique.
  • If a route fails, change one behavior at a time so the result is easier to interpret.
Verified fact

At 10/10, focus on the stated finish

The official objective pairs finding 10 Medkits with escaping. We do not publish a fixed exit location or an exact final sequence because those details were not confirmed by the official sources reviewed.

Recommendation: once your counter reaches 10, follow the live game prompts and prioritize a route you can recognize over extra exploration.
